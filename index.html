Contest Gifts Distribution · The Logic
Gifts are earned, not given.
Across three periods the bar keeps rising. Branches unlock Normal Gifts only by clearing that period’s participation threshold — while the highest scorers take home the Special Gifts.
Three Periods · The Rising Bar
Period 120 – 25 Oct ’26
30%& above
Eligible participation for Normal Gifts
⭐ Special GiftsTop 10 scoring branches
Period 226 – 31 Oct ’26
60%& above
Eligible participation for Normal Gifts
⭐ Special GiftsTop 5 scoring branches
Period 301 – 08 Nov ’26
90%& above
Eligible participation for Normal Gifts
⭐ Special GiftsTop 3 scoring branches
🏆
Master Gift
Awarded to the single branch that holds the maximum total score points across the full contest.
Rules & Notes
1
Normal gifts are given only to branches that achieve the eligible participation % for that period — other branches receive no gifts for this contest.
2
Special gifts go to the Top 10 scoring branches in the first two periods, and only the Top 3 branches (by score) in the final period.
3
If several branches land on the same % or score, the gifts are split equally; where gifts fall short, a lucky-draw or overall-performance call decides the allocation.
4
The Master Gift goes to the single branch holding the maximum total score points — a tie is resolved by the method above.
Daily Participation Scoring · Score Card System
Every day, every branch — ranked.
Each day branches are ranked by participation. Rank 1 earns 14 points, stepping down by one to 1 point for Rank 14. Points accumulate through the contest to decide the Special and Master gifts.
